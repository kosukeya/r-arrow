# r-arrow

**r-arrow — Robustness of Observable Time Asymmetry under Coarse-Graining**

`r` stands for **Robustness**.

r-arrow studies which measurable signatures of temporal direction survive loss of information under coarse-graining in finite stochastic systems.

The project starts with a deliberately small research program: Stage 0 freezes the research question and success contract; Stage 1 establishes an exact reversible/irreversible benchmark; Stage 2 performs a finite coarse-graining survival census. Stage 3 and beyond are intentionally not fixed yet.

Core guard:

> observable irreversibility != ontological becoming
